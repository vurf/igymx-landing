Features

  • Workouts and plans

    Create routines and reuse training templates.

    Track each set with exact load and reps.

  • Rest timer

    Stay on pace between sets and exercises.

  • Workout history

    Review previous sessions in a few taps.

  • Progress and metrics

    Monitor trends in volume and strength.

  • Exercise library

    Use a clear catalog for key muscle groups.

  • Dark mode

    Comfortable UI during early or late sessions.

    Optional integration for workouts and fitness metrics.

FAQ

Is the app good for beginners?

Yes. Start with simple plans and grow over time.

Do I need internet during workouts?

Core tracking works locally on your device.

Can I edit past workouts?

Yes, you can update history entries and notes.

How does HealthKit sync work?

HealthKit is optional and only enabled with your permission in iOS settings.

Do you use HealthKit data for ads?

No. HealthKit data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

How can I delete my data?

Delete local data in the app and request deletion by email.

Do I need an account?

If no account is used, data is stored on the device by default.
