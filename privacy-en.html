Privacy Policy for iGym X

Last updated: 20.02.2026

We respect your privacy. iGym X stores and processes only data required for workout tracking features.

1. What data we process

  • Workout data: exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes, and rest intervals.
  • Progress and body measurement data if you enter it in the app.
  • Technical data required for stability and error diagnostics.

2. Apple Health / HealthKit

  • Apple Health (HealthKit) access is available only after explicit user permission.
  • You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS settings.
  • Only workout/fitness-related data required by enabled features is used.
  • HealthKit data is not used for advertising, is not shared with ad networks, and is not sold.

3. Data deletion

You can delete your data locally inside the iGym X app. You can also request deletion by email: support@igymx.ru.

4. Third-party sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for commercial advertising purposes.

5. Contact

For privacy requests: support@igymx.ru.
