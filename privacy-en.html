Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-01

1. In short

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • HealthKit access is requested only with user permission and can be revoked in iOS settings.
  • HealthKit data is used only to provide app functionality.

2. Data we may collect/store

  • User-entered workout data: exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration, notes.
  • Technical data: device model, iOS version, app version, crash logs (if present).
  • Optional anonymized usage analytics (if enabled).

3. Apple Health / HealthKit data

With user permission, the app may read/write workout-related data such as workouts, active energy, and body measurements.

  • No HealthKit data is used for advertising or data brokering.
  • No HealthKit data is shared with third parties except if strictly needed to operate the app (typically none).
  • HealthKit data is processed on-device by default.

4. How data is used

To deliver app features, improve stability, and provide customer support.

5. Sharing

No data selling. Minimal technical data may be processed by service providers (e.g., analytics/crash reporting). HealthKit data is not shared for these purposes.

6. Retention and deletion

If the app has no account system, data is generally stored on your device. You can delete data in-app and/or request deletion via support@igymx.app with subject “Data Deletion”.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

9. International users

Your data may be processed under applicable laws in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be available on this page.

11. Contact

Privacy and support contact: support@igymx.app.
