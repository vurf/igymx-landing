Privacy Policy for iGym X

Updated: <MONTH> <YEAR>

"iGym X: Strength Tracker" respects user privacy.

1. Data we process

The app may process data you enter: workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weight, notes, and related fitness metrics.

2. Apple Health / HealthKit

HealthKit access is optional and only enabled with your explicit iOS permission.

With permission, the app may read and/or write workout and fitness-related metrics within the scope you allow.

You can change or revoke Health permissions at any time in iOS settings.

We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, and we do not sell HealthKit data or any personal data.

3. Data storage

By default, data is stored locally on your device. If no account is used, data remains on-device.

4. Data deletion

You can remove local data using app controls or by deleting the app from your device.

You can also request deletion by email: support@igymx.ru.

5. Contact

For support and privacy inquiries: support@igymx.ru.
