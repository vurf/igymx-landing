Privacy Policy for iGym X

Updated: 19.02.2026

We respect your privacy and process only the data necessary for iGym X features.

1. What data we use

  • Workout data: exercises, sets, reps, weights, and rest times.
  • Notes and progress parameters if you add them manually.
  • Technical information for diagnostics and app stability.

2. Apple Health / HealthKit

  • Access to HealthKit data is granted only after your explicit permission in iOS.
  • You can revoke access at any time in iPhone settings.
  • HealthKit data is used only for app features that you enable.
  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or ad personalization.

3. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Storage and security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to reduce the risk of data leaks or unauthorized access.

5. Data deletion

You can delete your data in the app or send a deletion request to support@igymx.ru.

6. Contacts

For privacy questions: support@igymx.ru or Telegram.
