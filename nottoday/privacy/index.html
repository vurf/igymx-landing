Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how NotToday handles information when you use our mobile application.

Last updated: April 2026

Introduction

NotToday is designed to help users resist impulses, reduce harmful habits, and track saved money. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

Information We Collect

  • User-provided data: habit settings, reminder preferences, and optional notes entered in the app.
  • Local device data: app progress and preferences stored on your device to provide core functionality.
  • Technical data (minimal): limited technical signals may be processed if required for app functionality, stability, or troubleshooting.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain app features.
  • To send reminders that you enable.
  • To improve reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • To respond to support requests and feedback.

Notifications

NotToday may send local or push notifications if enabled by you. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Analytics (only if enabled)

We do not sell personal data. If analytics tools are not enabled, we do not use analytics tracking beyond minimal technical processing required for app functionality. If analytics are enabled in future versions, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Storage

Most app data is stored locally on your device. If cloud services are introduced in future versions, we will describe the storage provider, safeguards, and retention terms in an updated policy.

Third-Party Services

NotToday may rely on platform services provided by Apple (such as App Store delivery and notification infrastructure). Any additional third-party integrations will be disclosed in future updates to this policy.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing activities.

Data Deletion

You can delete app data through app settings when available, uninstall the app to remove local data from your device, or contact us to request assistance with deletion.

Children’s Privacy

NotToday is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date on this page.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at support@igymx.ru.
