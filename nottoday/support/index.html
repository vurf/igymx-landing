FAQ

How do reminders work?

You can set reminders inside the app. Notifications are delivered by your device and can be changed at any time in iOS notification preferences.

How do I reset my progress?

Open app settings and use the reset option for your tracking history. Please confirm carefully — this action may be irreversible.

How is saved money calculated?

Saved money is estimated from the cost values and frequency you configure. It's a motivational estimate, not financial advice.

How do I delete my data?

You can request data deletion from within the app when available, or contact us at support@igymx.ru.
